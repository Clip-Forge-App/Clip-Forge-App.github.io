Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Clip Forge ("we", "us") handles information when you connect your TikTok account to Clip Forge.

1. Information we collect

When you log in with TikTok and authorise Clip Forge, we receive from TikTok:

  • Your basic TikTok profile information (open ID, display name and avatar), via the user.info.basic permission.
  • Access and refresh tokens that let Clip Forge upload videos to your account, via the video.upload and, where granted, video.publish permissions.

We do not collect your TikTok password, contacts, messages, location or payment information.

2. How we use it

We use this information only to identify the connected account and to upload video clips that you have chosen to that account. We do not use it for advertising or profiling.

3. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your information with third parties, except with TikTok as required to perform uploads, or where required by law.

4. Storage and security

Tokens are stored securely and are used only by Clip Forge. We take reasonable measures to protect them from unauthorised access.

5. Retention and deletion

We keep your information only while your account is connected. You can disconnect Clip Forge at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services (or similar), or by emailing us. When you disconnect or ask us to delete your data, we delete stored tokens and profile information within 30 days.

6. Your rights

Under UK GDPR and similar laws, you can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, or object to its use. Email us to make a request.

7. Children

Clip Forge is not intended for anyone under 18.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version will always be on this page with its effective date.
